The *_COMPLETE.xlsx files contain the comprehensive information extracted from each condition-specific astrocyte–GABAergic neuron metabolic model (Control, Incipient, Moderate, and Severe). Each workbook includes the complete list of reactions, metabolites, genes, gene–reaction associations, reaction formulas, metabolic subsystems, lower and upper flux bounds, objective coefficients, exchange reactions, cellular compartments, and the non-zero coefficients of the stoichiometric matrix. These files provide a structured and accessible representation of the original genome-scale metabolic models and facilitate their inspection, comparison, and reproducibility across the different stages of cognitive impairment.
